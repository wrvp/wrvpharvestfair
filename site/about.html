About the Harvest Fair

The White River Valley Players’ annual Harvest Fair brings Rochester together for a day of food, crafts, music, kids’ activities, and the flower show on the bandstand — a community service sponsored by WRVP.

This year’s fair

Saturday, Sept. 12, 2026 · 10 a.m.–4 p.m. on Rochester Park. Vendor check-in is between 8 and 9:30 a.m.; checkout is 4 p.m.

Suggested donation $2; kids free.
