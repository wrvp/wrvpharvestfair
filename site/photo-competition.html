Photo Competition

We’re excited to see what you loved about this year’s Harvest Fair!

To participate in this year’s photo competition, please submit 1–3 photos taken at the Harvest Fair. We’d love to see photos that capture fair activities, the fair setup, and people enjoying and participating in the festivities. From close-ups to wide-angle shots, we want to see it all!

Ready to enter? Upload your photos using the form below.

The competition will close September 20th and the winner will be announced October 1st. Please note that photos submitted to the competition may be used to promote future WRVP Harvest Fairs. Any information you provide (name, phone number, and email address) will only be used to contact the competition winner.
