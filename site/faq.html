Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for vendors, volunteers, and fairgoers.

When and where is the fair?

Saturday, Sept. 12, 2026, 10 a.m.–4 p.m. on Rochester Park. Suggested donation $5; kids free.

What time do vendors check in?

Between 8 and 9:30 a.m. Checkout is 4 p.m. Plan to arrive early for set-up.

How do I reserve a booth?

Register online or download the PDF, then mail it with a check to Christine Masaba-Meagher, Harvest Fair, 466 S. Main St., Rochester, VT 05767. Checks payable to White River Valley Players.

What are the booth fees?

Fees depend on nonprofit vs for-profit and whether you demonstrate or sell. See the fee table on the vendor signup page. Early rates apply if paid before August 31.

How big are the booths?

Booths are at least 10×16 ft. Double booths are available at twice the fee.

Can I get electricity?

Request it on the form and list appliances and wattage. Availability may be limited.

I’m selling food — any guidelines?

Tell us exactly what you plan to sell so we can avoid duplication. It’s suggested you have a drink available with food.

How do I volunteer or donate?

Use Sign Up as Volunteer, or Donate (opens the WRVP donate page).
