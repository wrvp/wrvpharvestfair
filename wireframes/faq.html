Frequently asked questions

One purpose: answer common vendor and visitor questions before Contact Us.

When and where is the fair?

Saturday, Sept. 12, 2026, 10 a.m.–4 p.m. on Rochester Park. Suggested donation $2; kids free.

What time do vendors check in?

Between 8 and 9:30 a.m. Checkout is 4 p.m. Plan to arrive early for set-up.

How do I reserve a booth?

Register online or download the PDF, mail it with a check to Christine Masaba-Meagher, Harvest Fair, 466 S. Main St., Rochester, VT 05767.

What are the booth fees?

Fees depend on nonprofit vs for-profit and whether you demonstrate or sell — see the fee table on the vendor signup page. Early rates apply if paid before Aug 31.

How big are the booths?

Booths are at least 10×16 ft. Double booths are available at 2× the fee.

Can I get electricity?

Request it on the form and list appliances and wattage. Availability may be limited.

I’m selling food — any guidelines?

Tell us exactly what you plan to sell to avoid duplication. It’s suggested you have a drink available with food.

How do I volunteer or donate?

Use Sign Up → As Volunteer, or Donate in the nav (opens wrvp.org/donate).
